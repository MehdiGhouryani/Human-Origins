# A. afarensis S09 scale-reference draft

Status: **draft — not yet assigned to the CMS slot**.

This is a code-generated, class-A context schematic. It is deliberately not a generated fossil photograph or a facial/body reconstruction.

## Values used

- Stature: female average 105 cm and male average 151 cm, estimated from femoral lengths of 31 Plio-Pleistocene hominids (McHenry, 1991). The paper reports averages, not confidence intervals for these values.
- Sex-specific mass means: female 29 kg and male 45 kg (McHenry, 1992), using hindlimb-joint dimensions and human-derived prediction equations. The paper notes that ape-and-human proportion assumptions produce materially larger estimates.
- Lucy (A.L. 288-1): 20.4 kg, with a 95% prediction interval of 13.5–30.9 kg, from a separate convex-hull volumetric reconstruction (Brassey et al., 2018). This is one specimen-specific result, not a female species mean.

The diagram labels the different methods and prevents the Lucy interval from being mistaken for a species-wide sex range. It shows a 170 cm adult-human silhouette only as a scale reference.
